Why our bread is different

  • No yeast

    Only flour, water, salt and a living starter. The dough rises slowly on its own.

  • 48-hour fermentation

    Slow fermentation gives deeper flavor and keeps the loaf fresh for longer.

  • Local flour

    Grain from small farms near Almaty, milled fresh every week.

How we bake

  1. Feed the starter

    The evening before, we refresh the starter with flour and water.

  2. Mix and rest

    Dough is mixed by hand and rests for several hours.

  3. Shape and ferment

    Loaves are shaped and ferment overnight in the cool.

  4. Bake and deliver

    We bake at dawn and bring warm bread to your door.
